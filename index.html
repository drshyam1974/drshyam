Dr. SHYAM SUNDAR AGRAWAL

Biography

Dr. Shyam Sundar Agrawal has received Ph.D. from Sambalpur University for his research entitled "Neuro-Fuzzy-Genetic Algorithms Technique in Multy Criteria Decision Making". The thesis is regarded to be an exceptional and innovative contribution to knowledge in the Mathematics, Computer Science and Management Science. He has published good numbers of research papers in various international journals of repute and national and international conferences. He has more than twenty six years of experience in teaching and research. Currently he is working as an Assistant Teacher Mathematics in RANI RASHMONI HIGH SCHOOL (H.S.), 104/A S N BANERJEE ROAD, TALTALA, ENTALLY, KOLKATA, West Bengal 700014. He is Associated with Mathematics Olympiad and working for Mathematics Community. He has received State and National level Awards in Mathematics.
